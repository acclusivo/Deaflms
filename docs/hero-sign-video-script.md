# Deaf LMS: Official Hero Banner Welcome Sign Video Script

> **Video Title:** Welcome to Deaf LMS — Visual Learning for Deaf Children & Families  
> **Placement:** Top-of-Funnel Homepage Hero Banner Video (`app/page.tsx`)  
> **Primary Dialect:** Nigerian Sign Language (NSL) / International Sign (ASL-cognate roots)  
> **Target Duration:** 65–75 Seconds  
> **Key Objective:** Instantly reassure hearing parents, excite deaf children with visual gamification, and show that zero audio is needed for world-class learning.

---

## 1. Production & Filming Setup Guidelines

| Parameter | Specification | Purpose / Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Camera Framing** | Medium Shot (Waist to 8" above head) | Captures the entire **Signing Space** (navel to crown, shoulder-to-shoulder + 6" lateral reach). |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 Widescreen (1920x1080 or 4K @ 60fps) | High frame rate prevents motion blur on rapid fingerspelling hand transitions. |
| **Lighting** | 3-Point Softbox Setup + Ring Light fill | Eliminates harsh shadows under hands, chest, and jawline; highlights hand contouring. |
| **Backdrop** | Seamless Matte Neutral Navy or Deep Charcoal (`#0f172a`) | Provides maximum contrast for all hand skin tones and handshapes. |
| **Presenter Wardrobe** | Solid jewel tone (e.g., emerald green or rich burgundy) | No stripes, busy patterns, jewelry, long earrings, or reflective fabrics that distract eye gaze. |
| **Presenter Profile** | Native/Fluent Deaf Educator or Certified NSL Interpreter | Authentic non-manual features (NMFs), warm facial expressions, natural signing cadence. |
| **On-Screen Captions** | 24pt Bold Sans-Serif, high-contrast box overlay | 100% WCAG 2.2 AAA readable for bilingual deaf learners and hearing family members. |

## 2. Scene-by-Scene Video Script

### Beat 1: Warm Greeting & Sense of Belonging (0:00 – 0:12)
* **Visual Action & Staging:**
  - Presenter smiles warmly directly into the camera lens.
  - Performs the universal open-hand sweep for **WELCOME** bringing open-5 palms inward toward the chest.
  - Identifies themselves as a Deaf educator and gestures openly to include the viewer's entire family.
* **NSL Sign Gloss:**
  ```text
  HELLO! WELCOME [two-handed open sweep inward] DEAF L-M-S.
  I [point chest] DEAF TEACHER.
  YOU [point viewer] AND FAMILY [both hands 'F' circle out] BELONG HERE!
  ```
* **Non-Manual Features (NMFs / Facial Grammar):**
  - Radiant, hospitable smile, soft eye contact, gentle welcoming nod.
* **On-Screen Open Captions (English):**
  > *"Hello and welcome to Deaf LMS! I am your teacher. You and your family belong right here."*
* **Spoken Voiceover (for hearing parents / screen-readers):**
  > *"Welcome to Deaf LMS—the visual learning platform created especially for deaf children and their families."*

---

### Beat 2: Empathy for Hearing Parents & Zero-Audio Reassurance (0:12 – 0:25)
* **Visual Action & Staging:**
  - Presenter transitions into an empathetic posture, mimicking a worried parent pondering: *"How can I teach my child without sound?"*
  - Immediately waves hands in a gentle dismissive *"Don't worry!"* gesture.
  - Points to the eyes with two index fingers and gives a confident affirmative nod.
* **NSL Sign Gloss:**
  ```text
  HEARING PARENTS [mime listening, furrow brow] WORRY:
  HOW TEACH DEAF CHILD? SOUND NONE?
  DON'T-WORRY! [two hands push down gently, smile]
  EYES [point eyes] POWERFUL! VISUAL LEARNING BEST!
  ```
* **Non-Manual Features (NMFs / Facial Grammar):**
  - Empathetic furrowed brow relaxes into reassuring, warm eyes; affirmative, confident chin nod.
* **On-Screen Open Captions (English):**
  > *"Hearing parents often worry: 'How do I teach my child without sound?' Don't worry. Visual learning is powerful."*
* **Spoken Voiceover (for hearing parents / screen-readers):**
  > *"If you are a hearing parent wondering how to teach your child without sound, take heart. Your child's visual mind is extraordinary."*

---

### Beat 3: Interactive Fingerspelling Keyboard Demo (0:25 – 0:40)
* **Visual Action & Staging:**
  - Lower-third Picture-in-Picture (PIP) screen-capture animates onto screen left, showing the **Deaf LMS Fingerspelling Keyboard Trainer**.
  - Presenter points to the graphic, rapidly and crisply fingerspells `A - B - C - D`, then mimes typing on a QWERTY board.
  - Bursts into delight with the "Star Reward" hand flutter (sparkling fingers).
* **NSL Sign Gloss:**
  ```text
  LOOK! [point screen left] KEYBOARD TRAINER!
  LETTER A - B - C - D MATCH REAL HAND-SHAPE [sign A-B-C-D crisply].
  CHILD TYPE [mime keyboard], LEARN SPELL, WIN STARS [fingers flutter like stars]!
  ```
* **Non-Manual Features (NMFs / Facial Grammar):**
  - Wide engaged eyes, playful mouth movement (morpheme *'oo'* for precision), joyful reaction to stars.
* **On-Screen Open Captions (English):**
  > *"Our fingerspelling keyboard connects letters directly to real sign handshapes with fun typing games and star rewards!"*
* **Spoken Voiceover (for hearing parents / screen-readers):**
  > *"With our interactive keyboard trainer, children connect letters to real sign handshapes, building typing speed and literacy step-by-step."*

---

### Beat 4: Dual-Pane Video Storybooks & Nigerian Curriculum (0:40 – 0:54)
* **Visual Action & Staging:**
  - Screen PIP switches to the **Dual-Pane Interactive Storybook** (`StorybookReader.tsx`).
  - Presenter holds hands like an open storybook, points left to the illustrated page, then right to the continuous sign teacher video.
  - Gives a proud thumbs-up for Nigerian Sign Language (NSL) priority curriculum.
* **NSL Sign Gloss:**
  ```text
  STORY-BOOK [open hands like book] TWO SIDES:
  LEFT SIDE [point] PICTURE READ, RIGHT SIDE [point] TEACHER SIGN VIDEO.
  NIGERIA SIGN LANGUAGE [sign N-S-L + thumbs up] READY!
  CHILD READ HAPPY!
  ```
* **Non-Manual Features (NMFs / Facial Grammar):**
  - Animated narrative facial expressions; rhythmic body sway highlighting the two-sided layout.
* **On-Screen Open Captions (English):**
  > *"Explore interactive storybooks with side-by-side sign videos, tailored for Nigerian Sign Language (NSL) and bilingual reading."*
* **Spoken Voiceover (for hearing parents / screen-readers):**
  > *"Our dual-pane storybooks place fluent sign language videos right alongside illustrated pages, making reading joyful and natural."*

### Beat 5: Instant Free Lesson & Call to Action (0:54 – 1:10)

  ```text
  BELOW [point downward] WATCH FREE FIRST LESSON NOW!
  LOG-IN NOT-NEED!
  WANT MORE? REGISTER FREE!
  WE LEARN TOGETHER! [sign I-LOVE-YOU 🤟 + double thumbs up 👍👍]
  ```
* **Non-Manual Features (NMFs / Facial Grammar):**
  - Radiant smile, encouraging forward head nod, direct gaze holding the final frame for 2 seconds.
* **On-Screen Open Captions (English):**
  > *"Watch our free basic signs right below—no login needed! When you're ready, create a free account and let's learn together."*
* **Spoken Voiceover (for hearing parents / screen-readers):**

## 3. Post-Production Editing & Delivery Checklist

1. **Color Grading:** Boost contrast slightly so skin tones and hand silhouettes pop cleanly against the `#0f172a` backdrop.
2. **Audio Mix:** If using the optional voiceover, ensure the spoken narration is warm, mid-tempo (125 words/min), with zero intrusive background music.
3. **Pacing & Trimming:** Ensure no awkward cut-offs in signing strokes; let the final ILY sign rest on screen for a full 2-second freeze beat before the outro card.
4. **Thumbnail Poster Image:** Frame grab of Beat 1 (Presenter with open welcoming hands and a bright smile) overlaid with text: *"🇳🇬 Welcome to Deaf LMS • 100% Visual Sign Learning"*.
5. **Video Embed URL:** Upload to YouTube / Supabase CDN with parameters `rel=0&modestbranding=1&playsinline=1`.
