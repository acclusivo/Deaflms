# USER_TESTING.md — DeafLMS Observed Discovery Evidence

> **ACE Pitch Competition Evidence Standard:**  
> This document records actual qualitative observations from early discovery testing with Deaf learners.  
> It does not invent positive results, claim widespread clinical validation, or expose child identities.

---

## 1. Ethical & Safeguarding Principles

1. **Child Identity Protection:** All participants are assigned alphanumeric codes (`DL-01`, `DL-02`). No real names, photographs, or identifying data are shared.
2. **Data Minimization:** No medical, audiological, or biometric sign-language records are captured or stored.
3. **No Automated Cognitive Diagnosis:** Difficulties observed during learning tasks are treated as instructional guidance for human facilitators, not cognitive or neurological assessments.
4. **Honest Reporting:** Product iterations awaiting formal retesting are strictly marked as **"Awaiting retest"**.

## 2. Qualitative User Testing Logs

### Participant Log: `DL-01`
* **Age & Profile:** 16-year-old adolescent Deaf learner.
* **Communication:** Primary visual communication / Nigerian Sign Language.
* **Session Task:** Learn computer hardware components, connect signs to physical device pictures, and type spelling on a laptop.
* **Expected Behaviour:** Watch sign demonstration, select webcam picture, read definition, and spell W-E-B-C-A-M on physical keyboard.
* **Observed Behaviour:**
  - Strongly preferred using a laptop over paper notebooks; expressed enthusiasm for keyboarding.
  - Can identify computer pictures, icons, and diagrams rapidly.
  - When asked to write the vocabulary word in a physical exercise book, showed marked resistance and disengagement.
  - Struggled to spell words correctly outside the computer context.
  - Recognized the webcam photograph immediately, but had difficulty understanding its English written meaning when sentences contained complex or abstract vocabulary.
* **Identified Learning Gap:** Clear disconnect between **Picture Recognition**, **Spelling**, and **Written Meaning Comprehension**.
* **Product Iteration Made:**
  - Structured the vocabulary loop into 5 explicit stages: *Watch Sign -> Select Picture -> Learn Word -> Simple Meaning -> Practise Spelling*.
  - Rewrote definitions into short, familiar sentences (e.g., *"A webcam is a small camera connected to a computer. It lets people see you during a video call."*).
  - Integrated visual QWERTY keyboard trainer with letter-by-letter touch feedback.
* **Retest Result:** **Awaiting retest.**
* **Remaining Unanswered Question:** Does repeated tactile physical keyboard practice increase word retention and spelling accuracy when writing outside digital screens?

### Participant Log: `DL-02`
* **Age & Profile:** Approximately 10-year-old Deaf learner (Primary 4).
* **Communication:** Visual sign learner.
* **Session Task:** Multi-day vocabulary retention and concept matching across study sessions.
* **Expected Behaviour:** Retain previously introduced sign-meaning pairs across consecutive study days.
* **Observed Behaviour:**
  - Experienced visual working memory fatigue and retention lapses between sessions.
  - Needed repeated visual sign-to-picture matching prompts before recalling the simple meaning of the word.
  - Hesitated when confronted with dense paragraphs or multiple instructions presented simultaneously.
* **Identified Learning Gap:** Working memory retention between discrete learning sessions; susceptibility to cognitive overload from multi-step text prompts.
* **Product Iteration Made:**
  - One instruction per screen rule implemented.
  - Diagnostic feedback loop automatically recommends repeating sign-to-picture matching if an initial match fails.
  - Removed all numerical grading or timed countdown pressure.
* **Retest Result:** **Awaiting retest.**
* **Remaining Unanswered Question:** What is the optimal micro-practice spacing (e.g., 3-minute vs. 5-minute daily sessions) to maximize visual vocabulary retention without inducing fatigue in younger primary learners?

## 3. Protocol for Forthcoming School Pilots

As part of the planned institutional pilots with special education centres:
1. **Parental / Guardian Consent:** Formal written consent prior to onboarding.
2. **Pre-Test Baseline:** Recording learner baseline recognition of the 4 hardware words (Sign, Picture, Word, Spelling, Meaning).
3. **Structured Post-Test:** Testing retention at 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days post-intervention.
4. **Independent Facilitator Review:** Deaf educators evaluating whether the 5-step progression reduced teacher repetition time.
