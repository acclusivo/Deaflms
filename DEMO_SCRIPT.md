## 3. Contingency Handling & Judge Q&A Defense

### Contingency 1: What if a judge asks: "Does your AI invent new signs?"
* **Answer:**  
  > *"Never. All sign language videos in DeafLMS are reviewed and approved by human Deaf educators. Our AI tutor, 'Signy', strictly answers questions based on our approved curriculum. When a user asks about an unreviewed term, the AI explicitly declines to invent a sign and directs them to an approved local facilitator."*

### Contingency 2: What if the internet disconnects during the pitch?
* **Answer:**  
  > *"The entire competition demo runs locally on Next.js. All 4 computer lessons, verified photos, and offline fallbacks are pre-bundled and operational."*

### Contingency 3: What if a judge asks: "Why did you remove written spelling drills and facilitator diagnostics from the child demo?"
* **Answer:**  
  > *"In our field tests with Deaf children in Nigeria, we found that forcing early typing drills or diagnostic teacher charts interrupted their intuitive learning flow. Deaf learners absorb knowledge fastest when guided eye-first: see the physical object, see how human Deaf teachers sign it, understand its purpose, and test comprehension visually. Teacher analytics belong in the dedicated educator portal, while the child experiences pure, empowering gamified mastery."*
